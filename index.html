OKB Registration Portal

Register a waterway by sketching its alignment directly on the map or importing a KMZ/KML file. The curve is smoothed and the evenly spaced points are recreated as you draw — a stretch as a line, an enclosed body of water as an area.

How this page works — read me firstGuide
  1. Say what you are submitting in step 00. Registering puts a waterway on the map for the first time; mapping an existing one draws the line for a name the registry already holds; extending adds more line to a waterway that is already mapped.
  2. Work down the boxes. Region, Site, Type and Name can each be typed into or opened as a list. Each one narrows the next, so by the time you reach Name only the waterways that could be the right one are offered.
  3. Draw the alignment on the map. Press Start Plotting and click along the channel. The curve is smoothed and the evenly spaced points are recreated as you click — there is nothing to press afterwards.
  4. Right-click on the map removes the last point. Edit Original Points lets you drag a point to move it, or right-click one to delete it.
  5. To register an enclosed body of water — a pond, a catch basin, a retarding structure — switch the shape to Area and close the ring by clicking the first point again.
  6. Or import instead. A KMZ or KML file can be dropped beside the Name box in step 04, and you can pick which lines inside it to bring in.
  7. Point Interval in step 05 sets how far apart the recreated points sit. 20 m suits most river work.
  8. Submit. The registration is reviewed before it reaches the main file; you will be emailed at your official DPWH address when it is decided.

A dredging stretch is a line, not an area. Only draw an area when the thing being registered really is enclosed. Everything that will be reported on as a stretch — a river, a creek, a drainage run — must be drawn as a line, or the stations along it cannot be measured.
